Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Little Pages ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit smartfilemanger.github.io/little-pages/, use an account, or access our printable learning resources. Please read it with the Terms & Conditions below.

For parents and guardians: Little Pages is designed for families and children's learning. Accounts should be created and managed by an adult parent or guardian. Please do not submit a child's full name, personal email, phone number, photograph, school, precise location or other identifying information through the website.

1. Information we may process

  • Account information: If you choose to sign in, we use the email address you provide to request and verify a one-time login code. Our authentication provider may create and maintain account identifiers and authentication records.
  • Session information: Authentication tokens or session data may be stored in your browser so that sign-in can continue between page visits. You can sign out and clear your browser site data to remove locally stored session information.
  • Technical information: The website host, content delivery services and your browser may process routine technical data such as IP address, device/browser details, request time and pages or files requested in server or security logs.
  • Messages: If you contact us, we will process the details and contents you choose to send so that we can respond.

We do not intentionally ask for payment-card details on this website. The current cart is a demonstration and does not process payments. We do not currently describe or operate a behavioural advertising or analytics programme through this page.

2. Why we use information

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the website, authenticate adult users, make requested resources available, respond to enquiries, prevent misuse, troubleshoot technical problems and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Service providers and sharing

Little Pages is hosted using GitHub Pages. Email authentication is integrated with Supabase. The website also loads the Supabase client library from jsDelivr. These providers may process technical or account information as needed to deliver their services and under their own terms and privacy documentation. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or our rights.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The site may use browser storage required for the sign-in session and basic website operation. The hosting or service providers may use their own technical mechanisms. You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser settings; clearing them may sign you out or affect site functionality.

5. Children's privacy

Parents, guardians and educators should supervise children's use of the website. Children should not create accounts or send personal information themselves. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the address below so we can review the request and take appropriate steps, subject to applicable law.

6. Retention and security

We and our service providers retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, security, dispute handling and legal requirements. We use service providers and reasonable technical measures, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to create an account and can still browse public pages and available free resources. You can sign out and clear browser site data. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion or other handling of personal information. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. External websites

Links to third-party websites or services are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not by Little Pages. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.
